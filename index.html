sata-spindown-guard

Cold-storage disks that stay cold

Cron-friendly Bash guard that spins down idle, unmounted SATA HDDs with hdparm, keeps them down, logs and rotates, and handles several disks. A safety layer for cold-storage drives.

What it does

  • Only touches unmounted disks in active/idle state
  • Multi-disk with per-disk idle thresholds
  • Log rotation and an optional wake-up mode
